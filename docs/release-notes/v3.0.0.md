## AK Knockout Studio 3 — free video and photo editor, now on Android too

### Downloads

| File | For |
| --- | --- |
| `AK-Knockout-Studio-Setup-3.0.0.exe` | **Windows installer (recommended).** Desktop shortcut, Start menu entry and uninstaller. |
| `AK-Knockout-Studio-3.0.0-portable.exe` | Windows, nothing to install. Runs from a folder or USB stick. |
| `AK-Knockout-Studio-3.0.0.apk` | **Android phones and tablets** (Android 7 or newer). |

**Windows:** if "Windows protected your PC" appears, click **More info → Run anyway** (the app isn't signed with a paid certificate).
**Android:** open the APK on your phone. If asked, allow your browser or file manager to **install unknown apps**, then tap **Install**.

### New in version 3

- **Start screen:** choose **Video**, **Photo** or **Collage** when the app opens, like a phone editor.
- **Drafts:** every project is saved automatically with a thumbnail, including copies of its videos and music.
- **AI upscaling:** make photos 2× or 4× bigger and sharper (Fast or High detail), or enlarge with AI while saving. Hold to compare with the original.
- **AI background removal:** cut people out of photos in one click, and out of videos on every frame. Put a blur (portrait mode), colour, gradient or picture behind, or keep it transparent.
- **Refine brush:** erase or restore any part of a cut-out by hand; also works on objects.
- **Photo editor:** auto enhance, 12 filters, adjustments, crop and rotate, text, stickers and pictures on top. Save PNG, JPG or WebP up to 8192 pixels.
- **Collage:** up to 9 photos in 24 layouts with spacing, rounded corners and border colour.
- **Stickers and emoji:** about 150 emoji plus 8 shapes in any colour.
- **Video:** crop clips, play clips backwards, auto enhance, AI background removal.
- **Android app:** the whole editor on your phone. Finished files are saved to *Documents/AK Knockout Studio* and offered in the share sheet.
- **Open source:** the full code is now in this repository under the MIT licence.

Everything from version 2 is still here: multi-clip editing, transitions, looks, overlays, green screen, music, voiceover, knockout text and high-quality export up to 4K.

All editing and AI run on your own device. Nothing is uploaded.
