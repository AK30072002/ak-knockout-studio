# AK Knockout Studio 3 — User Manual

Welcome to **AK Knockout Studio 3**, a free video and photo editor for Windows, Android and the web browser, made by AJAIKRISHNA.

Everything in this editor is free. There is no watermark, no account, no subscription and no "Pro" version. Your videos and photos never leave your device: editing, the AI tools and exporting all happen on your own computer or phone.

This manual explains every feature from start to finish. To get going quickly, read **Part 2: Quick start** and come back to the rest later.

## 2. Quick start

### Edit a photo in one minute

1. Open the app. On the start screen, click **Photo** and choose a picture.
2. On the right, click **✨ Auto enhance** for an instant colour fix.
3. Click **✨ AI upscale…**, choose **2×**, then **Upscale**.
4. Click **Save photo** (top right), then **Save photo** again in the window that opens. The picture goes to your Downloads folder.

### Make a video in five minutes

1. On the start screen, choose a **Video shape** (or leave **Same as my video**), click **Video** and select your clips and music. You can select several files at once.
2. On the timeline, click the small round **+** between two clips and choose **Cross dissolve**.
3. Choose **Text preset… → Bold caption** and type your words in the **Content** box on the right.
4. Press the **Space bar** to play it.
5. Click **Export video**, keep the recommended settings, and click **Start export**.

Your work is saved as a draft the whole time. Click the **AK logo** at the top left to go back to the start screen.

## 3. Installing and opening the editor

| Way | How |
| --- | --- |
| **Windows installer** (recommended) | Download `AK-Knockout-Studio-Setup-3.0.0.exe` from the GitHub Releases page and double-click it. It adds a desktop shortcut and a Start menu entry. |
| **Windows portable** | Download `AK-Knockout-Studio-3.0.0-portable.exe`. It runs without installing, for example from a USB stick, and opens a little more slowly. |
| **Android** | Download `AK-Knockout-Studio-3.0.0.apk` on your phone and open it (below). |
| **In the browser** | Open the online version in **Chrome** or **Edge**. Nothing to install. |

**If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC"**, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. This appears because the app isn't signed with a paid certificate.

### On Android

1. On your phone, open the GitHub Releases page and tap `AK-Knockout-Studio-3.0.0.apk` under **Assets** to download it.
2. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to **Install unknown apps** (this is needed for any app that doesn't come from the Play Store), then go back and tap **Install**.
3. Open **AK Knockout Studio** from your home screen.

The Android app has the same features as the Windows app, with these differences:

- **Saving:** finished videos, photos and project files are saved to **Documents/AK Knockout Studio**, and the share sheet opens so you can also send them to your Gallery, Google Drive, WhatsApp and so on.
- **Touch:** tap to select, drag with one finger to move text, stickers and overlays. Use the **Size** and **Zoom** sliders on the right instead of the mouse wheel. The panels are stacked: scroll down for the timeline, layers and settings.
- **Back button:** closes an open window, then returns to the start screen, then leaves the app.
- **Speed:** phones are slower than computers, so AI tools and high-quality export take longer. For long videos, export at HD or Full HD.
- Requires **Android 7 or newer**. Updating to a new version installs over the old one and keeps your drafts.

**Upgrading from version 1 or 2:** just install version 3. Old `.akks.json` project files open automatically as video projects. The last project you had open in the browser version appears in Drafts.

**Best experience:** the Windows app, or a current Chrome or Edge, on a computer. The Android app suits quick edits on the go. A computer with a graphics card makes the AI tools much faster, but they also work without one.

## 4. The start screen and Drafts

The start screen appears every time the app opens, and whenever you click the **AK logo** or **New** in the top bar.

### Create new

| Card | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Video** | Choose one or more videos, photos and music files. Videos and photos go onto the video track in order; music becomes audio tracks. |
| **Photo** | Choose one picture. The editor opens in photo mode, at the picture's own size. |
| **Collage** | Choose 2 to 9 photos. They are arranged in a layout automatically. |

**Video shape** sets the canvas for a new video: **Same as my video** (the first clip's own shape), **16:9 YouTube**, **9:16 Reels, Shorts**, **1:1 Square** or **4:5 Portrait**. You can change it later.

You can also **drop files onto the start screen**: videos start a video project, one picture starts a photo, and several pictures start a collage.

The links underneath offer an **Empty video project**, **Try the knockout demo** (the colourful "AK STUDIO" example), **Open a project file…** and **Keyboard shortcuts**.

### Drafts

Every project is saved automatically about a second after each change, like the drafts in a phone app. You never need to save by hand.

- Each draft shows a thumbnail, its type (Video, Photo or Collage), its size or length, and when it was last edited.
- **Click a draft** to open it.
- **Duplicate** makes a copy, for example to try a different version.
- **Delete** removes the draft. Your original files on the computer are never touched.

**Drafts keep a copy of your videos and music** inside the app's own storage, so a draft reopens complete, even if you moved or deleted the original files. Files larger than 1.5 GB are not copied; reconnect those with **Find the files…** (section 25). The line under the drafts shows how much space they use. Deleting drafts frees that space.

**Back to my project** (top right of the start screen) returns to the project you were editing.

## 7. AI upscaling

AI upscaling makes a picture bigger **and** sharper. Instead of just stretching the pixels, an AI model (ESRGAN) that has learnt what real details look like redraws the picture at the larger size, so edges, hair and text come out much crisper than with normal resizing.

### Upscale a photo

1. Select the photo (or a picture overlay).
2. Click **✨ AI upscale…** in **AI tools**.
3. Choose:
   - **Scale**: **2×** (double size) or **4×** (four times bigger). The window shows the result size, for example 1000 × 750 → 2000 × 1500.
   - **Quality**: **Fast**, or **High detail** (slower, finer detail).
   - **Run on**: **Automatic**, **Graphics card** or **Processor**.
4. Click **Upscale**. A progress bar shows the time left. The first run takes a little longer while the AI model loads.

When it finishes, the photo is replaced by the bigger, sharper version. In a photo project the canvas grows too, so **Save photo** at "Original size" saves the upscaled picture.

- **Hold to compare**: press and hold to see the original; let go to see the result.
- **Undo upscale** goes back to the original picture. Ctrl + Z also works.
- You can upscale more than once (2× then 2× again = 4×), up to 8192 pixels on the longest side.

### Upscaling while saving

You don't have to upscale first. In the **Save photo** window, choose a bigger size such as **2× bigger**, **4× bigger** or **4K**, and leave **Enlarge photos with AI** ticked. Every photo is enlarged with AI, while text, stickers and shapes are drawn freshly at full size, so everything is sharp.

### How long it takes

The AI runs on your computer. With a graphics card, a typical photo takes a few seconds for 2×. Without one, it runs on the processor and can take from several seconds to a few minutes for large photos or 4×. You can **Cancel** at any time.

**Tip:** for very large photos, crop first. Upscaling is limited to 8192 pixels on the longest side.

## 8. AI background removal and cut-outs

### Remove the background from a photo

1. Select the photo (or a picture overlay).
2. Click **Remove background** in **AI tools**. The AI finds the **people** in the picture and removes everything else.
3. Open **Cut-out** to fine-tune:
   - **Edge tightness**: raise it if bits of background remain; lower it if parts of the person disappear.
   - **Edge softness**: a softer or crisper outline.
   - **Refine with brush…**: fix anything by hand (below).
   - **Remove cut-out** brings the whole photo back.

### Choose what goes behind

Under **Behind** in the Cut-out section:

| Choice | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **Clear** (photos) / **Black** (videos) | Nothing behind. In photos the background is see-through, so a PNG or WebP keeps the transparency. |
| **Blur** | The original background, blurred: a **portrait mode** effect. Set **Blur amount**. |
| **Colour** | A plain colour. |
| **Gradient** | Two colours blending. |
| **Picture** | Any picture you choose, such as a beach or studio backdrop. |

### Refine with the brush (and erase by hand)

**Refine with brush…** (or **Erase by hand…** before using the AI) opens a large view of the photo. A checkerboard shows what is removed.

- **Erase** removes what you paint over; **Restore** brings it back.
- **Size** and **Softness** set the brush. Tick **Show removed area in red** to see everything.
- **Undo stroke** takes back the last stroke; **Remove nothing** starts over.
- Click **Apply** when you're happy.

Because you can erase by hand from scratch, the brush also works on **objects, animals and products**, not only people.

### Remove the background from a video

1. Select a video clip on the video track, or a video overlay.
2. Open **Remove background (AI)** and tick **Remove the background behind people**.
3. For a clip, choose what goes **Behind** (Blur, Colour, Gradient or Picture).

The AI works on every frame. The preview may lag a little behind the picture; the exported video is worked out exactly, frame by frame.

**Example: put yourself in front of another video.** Put the background video on the video track, add the video of yourself as an overlay with **Full screen**, then tick **Remove the background behind people** on the overlay.

### Remove a colour (green screen)

For footage filmed against a green or blue screen, colour removal is cleaner than the AI:

- **Clips and photos**: in **Cut-out**, tick **Also remove a colour (green screen)**.
- **Overlays**: open **Remove a colour (green screen)**.

Choose **Green**, **Blue**, **White** or **Black** (or any colour), then adjust **Strength** and **Edge softness**.

## 9. Crop and rotate

Crop works on photos, collage photos and video clips.

1. Click **Crop and rotate…** (photos) or **Crop** (video clips), or press **C**.
2. Choose a shape: **Free**, **Original**, **1:1**, **4:5**, **9:16**, **16:9**, **3:2**, **2:3** or **4:3**.
3. Drag the **corners or edges** to resize the box, and drag the **middle** to move it. The grid helps with the rule of thirds.
4. **↺ 90° / ↻ 90°** turn the picture; **Straighten** fixes a tilted horizon (it zooms in slightly so there are no empty corners).
5. Click **Apply**. **Reset** goes back to the full picture.

In a photo project the canvas changes to the cropped size. The original is never lost: open the crop window again at any time to change it.

## 10. Collages

1. On the start screen, click **Collage** and choose 2 to 9 photos.
2. Click an empty area (or press **Esc**) to see the **Collage** settings on the right:
   - **Layout**: click any of the layout pictures. The choice depends on the number of photos.
   - **Shape**: 1:1, 4:5, 3:4, 9:16, 16:9 or 2:3.
   - **Spacing**: the gap between photos (0 for no gaps).
   - **Rounded corners** and **Border colour**.
   - **Add photos…** and **Shuffle photos**.
3. **Click a photo** in the collage to select it. Then:
   - **Drag** it to move it inside its cell, and **scroll the mouse wheel** to zoom it.
   - Use every photo tool: filters, adjustments, crop, AI upscale and background removal.
   - **▲ / ▼** move it to the next or previous cell; **Duplicate** and **Delete** work too.
4. Add text and stickers on top, then **Save photo**. Collages are saved at 2160 pixels or more on the short side.

## 11. Stickers and emoji

1. Choose **＋ Add → Sticker or emoji** (or **Stickers** in the photo's Picture panel).
2. Pick a group: **Smileys**, **Love**, **Party**, **Hands**, **Travel**, **Food**, **Nature**, **Symbols** or **Shapes**.
3. Click a sticker. It appears in the middle of the picture.
4. Drag it into place and scroll the mouse wheel over it to resize. On the right you can rotate it, change its opacity, add a border or shadow, and more.

**Shapes** (heart, star, circle, arrow, speech bubble, burst, ring, tick) use the **Shape colour** chosen at the bottom of the sticker window.

In videos, stickers appear at the playhead for 3 seconds with a small "pop" animation; drag the ends of their orange bar to change that. Emoji look like your computer's own emoji style.

## 14. Trimming, splitting, speed and reverse

### Trimming

1. **Drag the edges** of a clip on the timeline, or
2. Move the playhead and press **I** (in point) and **O** (out point), or
3. Type exact times in the clip's **Trim** section.

**Reset** brings back the whole clip. Trimming never changes your original file.

### Splitting

Move the playhead, select the clip and press **S** (or **Split**). To **remove a middle part**, split twice and delete the piece in between. Split also works on text, overlays and audio tracks.

### Speed

In **Speed and sound**, drag **Speed** from **0.1×** to **8×**, or click **0.25×**, **0.5×**, **1×**, **2×** or **4×**.

### Reverse

Tick **Play backwards (reverse)** in **Speed and sound**. The clip plays from its end to its start, with its sound reversed in the exported video. Reversed clips can stutter a little in the preview because the editor jumps backwards frame by frame; the export is smooth. To reverse only part of a clip, split it first.

## 15. Transitions

1. On the **Video** row, click the round marker where two clips meet (**+** means none yet; a red **⇄** means one is set).
2. Choose a transition and its **Length** (0.1 to 3 seconds).

| Transition | Looks like |
| --- | --- |
| None (cut) | An instant switch |
| Cross dissolve | One clip fades into the next |
| Dip to black / Flash (dip to white) | Through black or a white flash |
| Push left / right / up / down | The new clip pushes the old one away |
| Wipe left / right | A moving edge reveals the new clip |
| Circle reveal | The new clip grows from a circle |
| Zoom | The old clip zooms towards you |
| Blur | The old clip blurs away as the new one comes into focus |

**Use on every cut** copies the transition to every join. The clips overlap during a transition, so each one makes the video slightly shorter.

## 16. Filters, looks and adjustments

Every clip and photo has its own colour settings.

- **✨ Auto enhance** looks at the picture and sets brightness, contrast, colour, warmth and sharpness for you.
- **Looks / Filters**: Original, Vivid, Punchy, Warm, Cool, Golden hour, Cinematic, Dreamy, Faded, Vintage, Black & white, Noir.
- **Adjustments**: Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, Warmth, Hue, Sharpen, Vignette, Film grain, Blur, Greyscale, Sepia, Invert.
- **Apply to all clips / photos** copies the settings to every clip or photo, so everything matches. **Reset** clears them.

## 17. Motion (Ken Burns) and framing

**Motion** (video clips): **Slow zoom in**, **Slow zoom out**, **Pan left / right / up / down**, with a **Strength** slider. Perfect for photo slideshows.

**Framing**: **Fill** covers the frame, **Fit** shows the whole picture, **Stretch** fills exactly. **Zoom**, **Move X / Y**, **Rotate** (with 90° buttons) and **Flip** position the picture. **Crop and rotate…** is here too. **Reset framing** undoes it all.

## 19. Background

Select **Background** in the Layers panel.

| Type | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Colour | A solid colour |
| Gradient | Two colours, linear (with angle) or radial |
| Image | Your own picture: Fill, Fit, Stretch or Tile |
| Blur | A blurred copy of your video, great behind vertical videos |
| None | No background (the default for new videos and photos) |

**How it works with knockout text:** the background covers your video or photo, and knockout text cuts holes in it so the picture shows only inside the letters. With **None**, everything shows and text sits on top. Adding the **Knockout title** preset when the background is None switches the background to black automatically, so the effect works straight away.

## 25. Project files and reconnecting media

Drafts (section 4) are the everyday way to keep your work. **Project files** are for backups and for moving a project to another computer.

## 27. Step-by-step recipes

### A. Make an old or small photo HD

1. **Photo** → choose the picture.
2. **✨ AI upscale…** → **4×** → **High detail** → **Upscale**.
3. **✨ Auto enhance**.
4. **Save photo** → **PNG** → **Original size**.

### B. Portrait-mode photo

1. **Photo** → choose a photo of a person.
2. **Remove background** → in **Cut-out**, **Behind** → **Blur**, **Blur amount** about 30.
3. **Save photo** → **JPG**.

### C. Profile picture with a coloured background

1. **Photo** → your photo. **Crop and rotate…** → **1:1** → **Apply**.
2. **Remove background** → **Behind** → **Gradient**, and pick two colours.
3. Fix any edges with **Refine with brush…**.
4. **Save photo**.

### D. Product photo with a transparent background

1. **Photo** → the product photo.
2. **Erase by hand…** → paint away the background with **Erase** (use a big brush, then a small soft one near the edges) → **Apply**.
3. **Save photo** → **PNG** → tick **Transparent background**.

### E. Instagram collage

1. **Collage** → choose 4 photos.
2. Press **Esc** → pick a layout, **Shape 4:5**, **Spacing** 20, **Rounded corners** 10.
3. Click each photo and drag it to frame it nicely.
4. **Text preset… → Elegant quote**, and a heart sticker.
5. **Save photo** → **JPG** → **Social media (1080)**.

### F. Vertical Reel from a landscape video

1. On the start screen pick **9:16 Reels, Shorts**, then **Video** and choose the video.
2. Select the clip → **Framing** → **Fit**. Select **Background** → **Blur**.
3. Add a **Bold caption** and music. **Export video**.

### G. Put yourself into another video (no green screen)

1. **Video** → choose the background video.
2. **＋ Add → Overlay** → your video of yourself → **Full screen**.
3. On the overlay, tick **Remove the background behind people**.
4. **Export video** (high quality works out every frame exactly).

### H. Rewind effect

1. Split the moment you want to rewind (press **S** at its start and end).
2. **Duplicate** the piece, select the copy and tick **Play backwards (reverse)**.
3. Add a **Flash** transition between them.

### I. Photo slideshow with music

1. **Video** → select all your photos and a song.
2. Give each photo **Motion → Slow zoom in** or a pan.
3. Click the first **+** between clips → **Cross dissolve** → **Use on every cut**.
4. Select the song → **End with the video**. **Export video**.

## 28. Troubleshooting and questions

**The start screen appears every time. Is my work lost?**
No. It's in **Drafts** on the start screen. Click it to continue.

**AI upscale or background removal is slow.**
The first use loads the AI model. After that, speed depends on your computer. Press **Esc** in an editor project and open **AI engine**: if a graphics card is listed, choose **Graphics card**. Crop large photos first, use **2×** and **Fast**, and close other heavy programs.

**Remove background left bits behind, or removed part of the person.**
Change **Edge tightness**, then use **Refine with brush…**. The AI recognises people; for objects, use **Erase by hand…**.

**The video background removal flickers in the preview.**
The preview shows the AI's latest result while it keeps up with playback. The exported video is calculated exactly on every frame.

**A reversed clip stutters.**
That's only the preview. Exports play smoothly.

**My video won't import.**
Convert it to MP4 (H.264) or WebM, for example with the free HandBrake app.

**Clips say "offline".**
Use **Find the files…** (section 25). Drafts usually avoid this by keeping copies.

**Drafts take up a lot of space.**
They keep copies of your videos. Delete drafts you no longer need on the start screen.

**My text is invisible.**
With **Video in text** mode and no background, the text shows the same picture it sits on. Choose **Solid** fill mode, or set a background.

**The export window doesn't list H.264.**
Use the Windows app, Chrome or Edge, or choose AV1 or VP9.

**Is anything uploaded?**
No. Videos, photos and the AI tools all run on your computer. Only the built-in fonts are downloaded from the internet.

## 29. What the editor does not do

Honest limits, so you know what to expect:

- **AI background removal recognises people.** For objects, pets and products, use **Erase by hand…** or a green screen.
- **AI upscaling is for photos and pictures.** Videos are upscaled with high-quality resizing plus sharpening, not AI, because AI on every video frame would take far too long on a normal computer.
- **AI upscaling improves detail but can't recover what was never there**, such as a face that is only a few pixels wide.
- **No automatic captions, object removal (inpainting) or AI voice effects.**
- **No keyframe animation** of settings over time; use Motion, transitions and the animations instead.
- **One speed per clip.** Split a clip to change speed part-way.
- **Reversed clips stutter in the preview** and their reversed sound is heard only in the export.
- **High-quality export can take longer than the video**, especially at 4K or with AI background removal.
- **Drafts live in this app on this computer.** To move a project to another computer, use **Save** for a project file and copy your media files with it.
